1. Scope

In this policy, “susmo,” “we,” “our” and “us” mean Shenzhen Dunlin E-Commerce Co., Ltd., the company that operates this website from Shenzhen, China. “Personal data” means information that identifies you or that can reasonably be linked to you.

Except as noted below, this policy applies to the personal data we process in connection with:

  • visitors who browse the pages of this website, including our product catalog, hair guide, FAQ and company pages;
  • people who email us about a style, a sample, an order placed elsewhere, or working with us;
  • the technical and security records needed to run and protect the website;
  • restricted use of our website management area by our own administrators.

It does not apply to records about our employees, personnel, job applicants or candidates, to information that privacy laws exempt, or to websites and services that we do not operate, including pages we link to and the email service you use to write to us.

Additional notices. If we ever publish a specific notice for a particular situation, for example a promotion run together with a partner, that notice applies to the personal data it covers and controls if it conflicts with this policy.

Other platforms. If you contact us through a social media platform or a marketplace, that platform handles your information under its own privacy terms. What we receive there is covered by this policy once it reaches us.

2. Personal data we collect

We collect very little, and deliberately so. This website is built to give information, not to gather it.

Information you send us by email

If you email us, we receive your email address, the message you write, any attachments and anything else you choose to include, such as your name or a style name. Your name, phone number or other details reach us only if you decide to include them. Please send only what your question needs, and please do not send identity documents, financial details, payment information or other sensitive information.

Email links on this website open your own email application. Messages travel through email services outside this website; they are not collected by a website form and are not stored in a customer profile.

Information collected automatically

Delivering a web page involves some technical information, including an IP address, the requested path, the time of the request, the request method and the response status and size. Our own web server access logs are configured to record only the request time, method, page path, status code and response size. They do not record IP addresses, full query strings, browser identification strings or request bodies.

Our hosting, network and security providers may process connection and request information as needed to deliver content, keep the service available and prevent abuse. Short-lived error or security records may contain more detail when it is needed to investigate a specific technical problem. We do not use these records to build visitor profiles, and we do not combine them with information about who you are.

Information we do not collect

  • There are no visitor accounts: no registration, sign-in, password reset or profile on this website.
  • There is no cart, checkout, payment, order or return process here, so we do not collect payment, billing or delivery information through the website.
  • There are no public comments, star ratings, customer reviews, newsletter sign-ups, enquiry forms, live chat or callback requests.
  • We do not buy visitor data, and we do not receive visitor profiles from data brokers, advertising networks or social platforms.

Administrator information

A restricted management area is used by our own administrators to maintain products, images and pages. Signing in requires credentials and creates authentication and security records. Those records belong to the running of the site and are separate from anything a visitor does; visitors have no accounts here.

3. Why we use information

We use the limited personal data described above only for these purposes:

  • Services and support. To display our catalog and company information, keep the website working, and answer the questions you choose to send us.
  • Security and protection of rights. To keep the management area restricted, prevent and detect misuse, fraud and unauthorized access, and protect this website, our visitors and our legal rights.
  • Analytics and improvement, in aggregate only. To diagnose faults and understand which pages are useful, using records that are not used to identify anyone.
  • Legal obligations and proceedings. To comply with applicable law, respond to valid legal process and handle privacy requests.
  • General business operations. To keep ordinary business records, and to plan for a possible transfer of the business as described in section 4.

We do not use visitor information for targeted advertising, cross-site behavioral profiling, automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects, loyalty or rewards programs, or unsolicited marketing. We do not send marketing email or text messages.

Where the law requires a legal basis for processing, we rely on our legitimate interests in operating and protecting this website and in answering the requests we receive; on your consent where we ask for it; and on legal obligations where they apply. Section 7 explains how to object to processing based on legitimate interests.

4. Disclosures of personal data

We do not sell personal data. We do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we do not provide it to data brokers. We disclose the limited personal data described above only in these situations:

  • Service providers. Providers that host and deliver this website or operate our email handle information on our behalf. For example, a hosting or network provider may process connection data, while an email provider processes the messages sent through its service. They are expected to use the information only to provide their service to us.
  • Legal requirements. We may disclose information when the law requires it, when we receive valid legal process, or where necessary to investigate misuse or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.
  • Protection of rights and safety. We may disclose information where we believe it is genuinely necessary to protect the rights, property or safety of our visitors, our company or others.
  • Business transfers. If susmo, or the business behind it, is sold, merged, reorganized or otherwise transferred, information we hold, such as email correspondence, may be transferred as part of that transaction and would remain subject to this policy and applicable law.
  • With your direction. We may share information in line with your specific instructions or your consent.

We may involve professional advisers, such as accountants, auditors or legal counsel, where needed, and public authorities where the law requires it. If you follow an external link or use your own email application, that service's own privacy terms also apply to what it does; we do not control how an independently operated service handles information.

5. Aggregate data

We may review aggregate figures about the use of this website, such as how many requests a page received in a month, to understand which information is helpful and to plan future content. These figures are compiled from operational records that are not used to identify individuals, and we do not attempt to re-identify anyone from them.

Where the law treats de-identified information in a particular way, we keep such information de-identified and use it only as permitted. If we share statistics, they will not identify any visitor.

6. Cookies, advertising and tracking

This website is deliberately quiet. We do not run advertising pixels, third-party marketing analytics, social media tracking, cart recovery tools or email open tracking, and we do not use cookies to profile visitors or measure advertising.

Necessary cookies

Restricted administrator sign-in uses the necessary authentication cookies required by our publishing platform. They are set when an administrator signs in to manage the site, not for visitors browsing the catalog. Browsing this website does not create a shopping session, a cart or a customer record.

Hosting or security services may use necessary technical mechanisms to deliver or protect the site, for example to keep a connection stable or to block abusive traffic. Those mechanisms are not used for advertising or profiling.

Your controls

You can block or delete cookies and local website data in your browser settings at any time. Blocking the necessary administrator cookies prevents administrator sign-in, but it does not affect browsing the catalog.

Because we do not sell or share personal data and do not run targeted advertising, there is no such activity on this website for a “do not sell or share” preference or a Global Privacy Control signal to switch off. If we ever introduced advertising or analytics, we would update this policy and offer a meaningful choice before doing so.

We do not collect commercial information, payment or financial information, precise geolocation, biometric data, audio or visual recordings through this website, professional or educational information, or sensitive personal information.

Sources. Directly from you when you email us, and automatically from your browser or device when it requests a page from this website.

Purposes and disclosures. As set out in sections 3 and 4: operating this website, answering your messages, security, legal obligations, ordinary business records, and disclosure to hosting, network and email providers and to authorities where required.

Sale and sharing. We do not sell personal information as the CCPA defines a sale, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We have no actual knowledge of selling or sharing the personal information of consumers younger than 16.

Retention. Email correspondence is kept only as long as needed for the purpose it was sent for, for our business records or to meet legal obligations. Technical records are short-lived operational data.

Your rights. Subject to the exceptions in the CCPA, you may ask to know what personal information we have collected and to obtain a copy of it; ask us to delete personal information; ask us to correct inaccurate personal information; ask us to limit the use of sensitive personal information, which we do not collect; and be free from discriminatory treatment for exercising these rights. Shine the Light. Under California Civil Code section 1798.83, California residents may ask once per calendar year for information about personal information we disclosed to third parties for their own direct marketing in the previous calendar year. We do not disclose personal information for third parties' direct marketing, so there is nothing to report; you are still welcome to send the request to the email address above.
